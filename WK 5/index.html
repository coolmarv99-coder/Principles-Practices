Opening vignette

“What Lens Are You Using?”

Jordan: “I thought we were done with theorists after Human Development.”

Maya: “Apparently, theorists follow us everywhere.”

Aaliyah: “But this chapter is using them differently. It’s not just, ‘Who said what?’ It’s more like, ‘How does this idea help us decide what to do with children?’”

Maya: “A theory can change what we notice. One teacher might see a child building with blocks and think about problem-solving. Another might notice language, social skills, imagination, culture, or emotional regulation.”

Aaliyah: “Program approaches are like that too. Montessori, Reggio Emilia, HighScope, Waldorf, Head Start…each reflects beliefs about children, teachers, families, materials, and the environment.”

Jordan: “So the real question is: What do we believe children are capable of, and how should that belief shape our teaching?”
