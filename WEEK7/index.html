Opening vignette

“Not a Race, Not a Checklist”

Jordan: “So which child is more advanced?”

Aaliyah: “That might not be the best question.”

Maya: “Milestones can help us understand what is typical, but they are not supposed to make us compare children like they’re in a contest.”

Jordan: “So the point is not, ‘Who is ahead?’ It’s, ‘What does this child need next?’”

Aaliyah: “Exactly. And we have to look at the whole child—physical, cognitive, language, social, emotional, cultural, everything.”

Maya: “Maybe good teachers learn to see development without rushing children through it.”

Jordan: “So, not a race, not a checklist.”

Aaliyah: “More like a map—a map that helps us support each child’s next steps.”
