Opening vignette

“Start With Strengths”

Jordan: “Working with children also means working with adults. And adults are complicated.”

Maya: “Families aren’t background information. They are children’s first teachers and know things we may never see in the classroom.”

Jordan: “What if a family is frustrated, overwhelmed, or disagrees with the teacher?”

Aaliyah: “That’s where professionalism shows up. We listen, communicate clearly, and preserve the relationship.”

Maya: “And start with strengths. Ask: What does this family care about? What pressures are they facing? What are they already doing well? How can we partner?”

Jordan: “So the goal isn’t to win an argument.”

Aaliyah: “The goal is to support the child by building trust with the family.”

Think first: How can educators build respectful partnerships when communication is difficult or perspectives differ?
