Opening vignette

“What Is the Behavior Telling Us?”

A child knocked over another child’s block tower and ran under a table.

Jordan: “My first thought was, ‘He knows better.’ But do I actually know what was going on?”

Maya: “Behavior is what we see, but it may not tell the whole story.”

Aaliyah: “Maybe he wanted to join and didn’t know how. Maybe it was too loud. Maybe he was overwhelmed or needed help with language, regulation, or problem-solving.”

Jordan: “So instead of starting with punishment, we start with curiosity?”

Maya: “Curiosity and guidance—not excusing the behavior, but understanding it well enough to teach what to do next.”

Aaliyah: “Protect others, set limits, and repair harm without shaming the child.”
