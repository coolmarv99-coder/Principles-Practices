Opening vignette

“What’s Your Why?”

Maya: “This chapter feels different. It’s not just asking what teachers do. It’s asking why we want to do this work.”

Jordan: “My first answer was, ‘Because I like kids.’ But now that sounds a little too simple.”

Aaliyah: “It’s a good starting point, though. Maybe the chapter is asking us to go deeper. Why young children? Why families? Why this kind of work, even when it’s tiring, messy, emotional, and not always appreciated?”

Jordan: “You had me until ‘messy.’ I’m still recovering from the glitter incident in lab.”

Maya: “But that’s part of it, right? The profession includes the sweet moments and the hard moments. We need to understand who children and families are, what teachers are responsible for, where we can work, when to keep growing, and how to become better over time.”

Aaliyah: “And maybe our ‘why’ helps us stay grounded when the work gets complicated.”

Maya: “So maybe becoming a teacher starts with a bigger question: What kind of difference do I hope to make?”

Think first: Why do you want to work with young children, and how might that reason shape the kind of teacher you become?
