Opening vignette

“Is This Curriculum?”

Jordan: “Okay, curriculum. Done.” He holds up a worksheet he found online.

Aaliyah: “Is that curriculum, or is that just a worksheet?”

Maya: “I used to think curriculum meant a list of activities. This chapter makes it more thoughtful than that.”

Aaliyah: “Curriculum is not just keeping children busy. It connects experiences to development, interests, families, culture, and learning goals.”

Jordan: “So for block building, we consider language, cooperation, counting, problem-solving, and motor skills?”

Maya: “And materials, teacher questions, evidence of learning, and what we might change next time.”

Aaliyah: “That’s the difference between an activity and intentional curriculum.”
